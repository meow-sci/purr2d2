# Cutting architecture

The current gameplay separates **Swing / Alt+F7** from **Select part to slice / Ctrl+Alt+F7**. The latter arms a cursor selection; clicking a part locks it and starts the original 0.62-second swing/recovery as soon as the blade is ignited. Animation does not wait for the worker to find a plane or prepare assets. `SaberRuntime.AssistedSlice` aims toward the clicked surface and extends the blade while background preparation tries up to nine candidate sections. Breakup requires both prepared native assets and presentation of the final main-view swing pose. It consumes one commit cue on the first subsequent safe update. Preparation failures are reported after the full stroke, not used to interrupt it.

`PartSliceTargeting` reuses KSA's current-frame `MainViewport.PartPicker` and normalized cursor ray for exact part selection. Hover validation does not scan fleet geometry. If no native pick exists, clicking starts an incremental two-sided triangle-ray fallback, without constructing a cold BVH or selecting a nearest-part substitute. Reach is measured from the kitten at selection, allowing a metre for the arm/hilt offset; it is not reimposed after the stroke. Ownership, crew and transform checks remain in place before native replacement. The flight-computer target does not constrain or cancel the cut. `SliceSourceCapture` copies model data in 256-triangle chunks with a 1.5 ms soft update budget. Geometry preparation and export are covered by [slicing research](research-slicing.md). Vessel mutation runs at the settled `PrepareFrame` seam after previous physics jobs have joined and before new jobs are dispatched.

`SaberContactProbe` is a separate read-only visual path. It emits sparks against static, dynamic and glass render meshes, including crewed targets. At 20 Hz it checks a cached contact triangle first, then uses a bounded resumable search of nearby mesh references. Native CPU arrays are read directly, with reusable candidate storage. The probe has no reference to the slicer and no destruction callback.

Autopilot, ring coverage, contact heat, sweep history and automatic connection/failure cutting have been removed. There are no persistent contact scars; only short spark bursts and the actual sliced fragments' cooling cut caps remain.

Managed validation covers incremental selection and commit guards, nonblocking worker cancellation, final-pose presentation, ready/late results, exactly-once completion, contact sampling limits, multi-primitive search continuation, stale-contact rejection and allocation-free sustained contact. Native rendering, spawning and save/load still require in-game acceptance.
