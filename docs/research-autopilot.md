# EVA cylinder cutting autopilot research

## Player workflow

The controller provides local MMU assistance for reaching a cutting ring. Its target dropdown lists vessels directly and writes the controlled kitten's stock `Vehicle.Target` through the settled command queue. Status text reports distance to the ring entry or remaining relative speed during flight. The player can match target velocity without capturing geometry or equipping a saber.

For cutting, **Find ring preview** searches the target's geometry without requiring the kitten to already be beside the correct cross-section. **Auto axis** tries the target body's X, Y and Z axes; manual axis and reversal remain available. Alternatively, **Select hull point** arms a scene click: the nearest target triangle along the click ray chooses an exact axial station. An invalid selected station is refused rather than replaced by a different station. The ring can then be moved by 0.25 m along its chosen axis; a failed nudge retains the previous preview.

The overlay draws the sampled hull ring in cyan and the MMU flight ring and entry point in amber. Far-side cut segments are dashed and faded. The amber line from the kitten to the entry shows the proposed direct approach. These are projected guidance marks, not depth-tested material cuts or evidence that the cutter can sever that location.

**Fly to ring** revalidates the selected station and preserves its approved entry marker. It takes MMU control, equips the saber with ignition and cutting inhibited, then approaches and holds. The player inspects blade orientation, uses the ±90° roll controls if necessary, and explicitly selects **Cut one orbit**. The blade must reach full deployment with a fresh rendered pose before the lap begins. Abort / release or Esc returns manual control; completing the lap or detecting structural separation also releases it.

## Geometry capture and ring search

`TargetSurfaceSnapshot` walks full parts and nested subparts. It prefers each part's declared `PartModelModule` render geometry and uses `MeshViewModule` picking geometry when no render mesh is declared. This avoids coarse or missing picking meshes hiding an otherwise available visible hull. Every `MeshReference.PositionsCompare` primitive is copied, not just the first primitive, and reused references are deduplicated only within the same part instance.

Vertices are transformed through the complete local scale/rotation/translation chain and then have `target.CenterOfMassAsmb` subtracted. The snapshot composes those matrices explicitly so scene picking does not populate the game's shared lazy transform caches while worker jobs run. Bounds are accumulated per full part. A captured `CutRing` stores its actual body-space center, signed axis, kitten-facing starting radial direction and sampled outer surface radius. Its axis can therefore pass through a cylinder offset from the vessel's center of mass.

For each permitted axis and candidate part bounds, automatic search evaluates up to six interior stations, including the kitten's projected station where possible. It samples 48 azimuth directions in three nearby axial planes, choosing the outer positive intersection so hollow walls do not become an inner-radius orbit. All sampled directions must intersect geometry; sampled radial variation must stay within both 0.15 m and 12% of the minimum radius. Among feasible candidates, automatic search favors the nearest entry. A selected hull point supplies only its exact projected station and favors the nearest candidate axis center. Automatic axis discovery is limited to the target-body axes; it does not fit an arbitrary tilted cylinder axis.

The flight radius is `surface radius + hull clearance`. Before accepting a ring, a BVH checks the straight approach as a capsule with a nominal 0.25 m kitten radius, then checks 96 segments around the whole flight ring. Each orbit capsule is inflated by the chord's sagitta to cover the corresponding curved arc. An obstruction refuses that candidate; automatic search may try another station. It does not compute a path around an obstacle.

This replaces two faults in the original native picking path: `Part.RayCastEgo` returned a subpart-local hit that was incorrectly treated as vehicle assembly space, and its MeshView/first-primitive path missed other visible geometry. The earlier capture also depended on the kitten already occupying an axial station intersecting the hull, making capture from beyond a cylinder's end fail.

## Native MMU control and moving targets

KSA suppresses general thruster flags for an EVA kitten outside `LocomotionMode.Mmu`. In MMU mode, `PhysicsBubble.FlightComputerInputsFor` consumes `Vehicle._manualControlInputs.ThrusterCommandFlags`. Purr2D2 writes that native seam, enables RCS and selects manual Pulse mode. It does not teleport the kitten or inject forces.

Native pulse commands request a thruster's minimum impulse at a cadence of at least 0.15 s. They are discrete commands, so the numerical acceleration demand is used to select direction rather than treated as a throttle. Available translation authority is estimated from active, fueled thrusters in all six directions, their intended forces, minimum pulse durations, the pulse cadence and kitten mass, with a conservative reduction. Approach guidance limits closing speed using braking distance, reserves 0.30 s for pulse latency, damps lateral drift and applies an actuator-scaled deadband. Its nominal approach speed cap is 1.5 m/s, reduced to 0.4 m/s during ring holding and cutting guidance.

Match target velocity cancels the difference between the kitten and target COM velocities in CCI, then releases below 0.045 m/s relative speed. Ring guidance instead subtracts `target angular velocity × kitten-relative position` before transforming relative velocity into target body space. The desired waypoint follows the captured offset center and target rotation; the cutting phase adds tangent velocity around that center.

Ring attitude uses the existing flight computer in Auto / ECL-body / Custom mode. The desired quaternion points the fresh blade direction radially inward and uses the cylinder axis as a secondary reference. Before the first rendered publication, the pose system's model-to-body direction provides a fallback. `FlightComputerRollMode.Up` couples roll so the whole custom attitude, including the ±90° choices, is tracked. Decoupled roll would ignore the roll component and could leave a blade that is not aligned with the control +X axis pointing incorrectly.

Flight engagement requires MMU locomotion, six-axis translation authority, no custom control part/connector, the same parent body and a nearby loaded vessel. The local envelope is a COM distance no greater than the target bounding radius plus 500 m and a COM relative speed no greater than 20 m/s. Ring guidance also requires target angular speed below 0.15 rad/s and a conservative bound on target-spin, orbit centripetal and Coriolis acceleration below 40% of estimated authority. Excessive spin, selected orbit speed or loss of propellant refuses engagement or releases the active controller.

## Timing, selection and ownership

UI actions queue commands for the checked `Program.PrepareFrame` insertion after stock input application and before the next solver dispatch. The host inspector validates this ordered IL seam and the private `Vehicle._manualControlInputs : ManualControlInputs` field. Geometry previews and picking do not mutate vessel structure.

The overlay runs after `Program.OnFrameViewports(double)` while the HUD is visible and uses the main viewport's camera projection with framebuffer-to-ImGui coordinate conversion. Point selection accepts a later left click outside UI windows/items in normal cursor mode. A prefix on `Program.OnMouseButton` consumes both press and matching release for the armed scene selection so stock targeting and camera clicks do not also act on it. Selection is cancelled if the controlled kitten or target changes, or on Esc; editor/world resets discard selection state.

The controller snapshots attitude mode/frame/tracker, custom target, manual-thrust mode, roll mode, RCS mode, rate limit and kitten control mode. Releasing clears translation flags before restoring those settings. The runtime separately restores the saber's previous equipped/ignited state after a ring flight. Match velocity alone does not take saber ownership.

Captured geometry is invalidated by changes to the target COM or recursive part identities/transforms. Engagement checks the original selection before rebuilding its geometry and refuses a different center or radius instead of silently relocating the ring. During every ring-controlled phase, the current recovery segment and conservative stopping segment are checked again; losing the corridor switches to velocity matching and inhibits cutting. Target loss/change, invalid navigation, leaving MMU, envelope/authority violations, world/controlled-kitten changes and shutdown release or reset control. Pause, unsupported warp and a zero simulation step clear translation commands. Simulation exceptions invoke guarded autopilot release before suspending the rest of the saber runtime.

## Work budgets and verification limits

Native snapshots allow at most 256 visited parts/subparts, 1,024 mesh instances, 8,192 primitives and 150,000 input triangles. Invalid/unavailable geometry refuses capture. The pure ring finder separately caps input at 200,000 triangles, 64 full-part candidate bounds, 1,536 candidates and four million narrow-phase triangle tests. Runtime route queries refuse more than 25,000 candidate triangles. Budget exhaustion reports a reason rather than treating skipped geometry as clear.

Managed tests exercise production ring geometry, surface snapshots, approach guidance and the controller state machine with game stubs. Coverage includes capture from beyond cylinder ends, offset centers, automatic/wrong axes, clicked station and nudge persistence, nearest picking, missing sectors, noncircular sections, obstructed approaches/orbits, pulse braking/holding, native flag signs and restoration/failure paths. The host checks inspect metadata/IL against the supplied KSA `2026.10.19.5638` assemblies; they do not execute the native game.

Native gameplay acceptance remains unperformed. Actual MMU response, physical kitten clearance, coupled attitude, moving-target tracking, click handling, overlay projection and end-to-end cutting still require in-game trials. Sampled circularity and capsule checks are geometric approximations: they do not prove watertightness, structural slice eligibility, sufficient blade reach, clearance from other vessels/terrain or collision-free dynamics under arbitrary disturbances. This is close-proximity assistance with direct-route refusal, not full orbital rendezvous, docking or general obstacle avoidance. A previewed ring can remain structurally uncuttable under the selected cutting mode.
